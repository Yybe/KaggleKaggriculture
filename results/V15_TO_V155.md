## 0. TL;DR (one paragraph)

V15 lost live matches because its fixed sell schedule dumps STRAWBERRY into *crashed* markets
(px 1–24) while opponents sell the same crops into *price-recovery* windows (px 47–85) just
before the terminal dump. V15.5 adds a single new pass — `_recovery_sell` — that computes
**dead inventory** (shed units the tape will never sell itself, by scanning `_ACTIONS`), and in
the **final window only (steps 705–716, price gate STR≥60/MILK≥110/WOOL≥200)** sells up to 12
dead units at the **front** of the market queue, capturing the day-30 recovery peak that the
step-717 terminal dump would otherwise destroy. Two earlier variants failed (0-40 and 18W/22L);
each failure revealed a sharper timing rule, and the final variant is **100% win rate** across
every test seed.

## 3. Step 2 — Root cause (the mechanism, not the symptom)

Two concrete defects, proven from the tape dump (`tape_dump.log`) and probes:

1. **Fixed-schedule dumps land in crashed markets.** V15 replays V14's `_ACTIONS` tape; the
   tape's STR sells cluster at 565/570/571/574/575/577/599/601/615/622/624/648/650/666/670,
   many landing when price is px 1–24. The tape's **last** STR sell is step 670 — anything still
   in the shed after 670 can only be sold at the terminal liquidation (step ≥716).
2. **Terminal liquidation sells after the glut.** `_terminal_liquidation` APPENDS STR at
   market-queue position 3 (after MILK/WOOL/WHEAT). In the lockstep engine, queue position =
   execution order. Opponents FRONT-LOAD STR at position 0, quoting against pre-glut inventory.

Key price-path facts from probes (seeds 2000, 2073):
- The terminal dump at 717 **always crashes price below the 713–716 peak** (seed 2000: px 70→32;
  seed 2073: px 158→120).
- Town shops drain inventory through 709–716, lifting price to px 62–85 — a genuine final window.

## 4. Step 3 — Candidate design (hypothesis)

`agents/kaggle_v155_recovery.py` = V15 + one new pass `_recovery_sell`:

- **Dead inventory** = `shed[item] − planned_now − Σ(SELL qty for item in _ACTIONS[step+1:])`.
  Fire only when `dead ≥ 1` AND current price ≥ gate. If the tape will sell the units itself,
  recovery never fires → behavior identical to V15 in that case (immune to the v1 failure below).
- Constants: `_RECOVERY_START`, `_RECOVERY_STOP=717`, `_RECOVERY_CHUNK=12`,
  `_RECOVERY_GATES={STRAWBERRY:60, MILK:110, WOOL:200}`, `_RECOVERY_PRIORITY=(STR,MILK,WOOL)`.
- Sells are front-inserted at market-queue position 0, capped at 10 market orders.

## 5. Step 4 — Failed variants & fallbacks (the honest part)

### Variant v1 — surplus front-loading (any step ≥ 570, no dead filter) → **0-40 vs both**
- Result: `h2h_v155_vs_v15.txt`, `h2h_v155_vs_v14.txt` — mean Δ −1444 / −1370.
- **Why it failed:** it stole volume from the tape's *own* 599/600/602 STR sells, crashed their
  prices, and handed the opponent the starved-market recovery.
- **Fallback fix:** added the dead-inventory filter — never sell units the tape will sell itself.

### Variant A — dead-inventory, window 570–716, gate STR≥60 → **18W/22L**
- Result: `h2h_v155_dead_vs_v15.txt` — mean Δ −421, median −185, min −1702, max +92.
- **Why it failed:** probes (seed 2073: STR held 154–161 @660, dipped 59–85 @671–674, recovered
  154–158 @709–716; V15 terminal-dumped 24 STR @ ~141 avg = 3,389 vs our early 22 STR @ ~68 =
  ~1,507) showed the 570–704 window sells into **mid-game dips** at px 51–68 while V15's
  terminal dump captures 120–141. The "recovery" mid-game is a fake-out — it crashes again.
- **Fallback fix:** moved the window to the FINAL recovery peak only: `_RECOVERY_START = 705`.

### Variant B — dead-inventory, FINAL window 705–716, gate STR≥60 → **40W/0L** (winner)
- Result: `h2h_v155_peak_vs_v15.txt` — mean Δ +492, median +553, min +147, max +645.
- Mechanism: selling dead STR at the 709–716 peak beats the terminal dump, which always crashes
  the price right after (seed 2000: px 70→32).

## 6. Step 5 — Verification protocol (how to prove the winner)

1. **Small gate test first** (always): 20 seeds × 2 seats = 40 games via `diag_bench.py`.
   Seeds = `2000 + s*73`, s=0..19. Win ≥ 90% + positive mean → proceed; else diagnose with probes.
2. **Fresh-seed robustness**: `v15_losses/diag_bench_offset.py` with offset 40 (seeds 4920–7767,
   never used in tuning) → `h2h_v155_peak_vs_v15_ext.txt`: **80W/0L, mean +472, min +118**.
3. **Baseline check**: vs V14 → `h2h_v155_peak_vs_v14.txt`: **40W/0L, mean +669** (V15's edge was
   +196 — 3.4× improvement).
4. **Beat the previous champion too**: vs stale champion_v155 → `h2h_v155_recovery_vs_champ155.txt`:
   **40W/0L, mean +632**.
5. **Post-promotion regression**: re-run the promoted file through the harness
   (`h2h_champ155_promoted_vs_v15.txt`): **20W/0L, mean +555** — promotion broke nothing.
6. **Submission bundle check**: rebuilt `submission.py`, self-play smoke + H2H
   (`h2h_submission_vs_v15.txt`): **20W/0L, mean +555** — identical to champion.

## 8. The replicable workflow (for creating the next best agent)

```
1. COLLECT EVIDENCE      Download replays of live losses → profile each opponent
                         (analyze_losses.py, price_analysis.py, revenue_breakdown.py).
                         Find the mechanism: per-step price path vs sell timing.

2. DUMP THE TAPE         Dump _ACTIONS to a log (tape_dump.log). Know EVERY future sell
                         the agent will make — "what's dead" is defined relative to it.

3. FORM ONE HYPOTHESIS   Change ONE lever per cycle. State the expected delta direction
                         and magnitude before running (pre-register, see DIAGNOSIS doc).

4. BUILD + SMOKE         Clone the champion, add the lever, smoke-test imports + 1 game.

5. GATE TEST (20 seeds)  diag_bench.py A B 20 out.txt --swap-both (40 games).
                         PASS = ≥90% win + positive mean. Else → probe the worst seeds.

6. PROBE FAILURES        probe_v155_dead.py style: per-step shed/price/revenue for both
                         agents on the losing seed. The probe must EXPLAIN the delta
                         down to ~1 sell order. Then fix the timing rule, not the trigger.

7. ROBUSTNESS (fresh)    diag_bench_offset.py with a seed offset never used in tuning
                         (≥40 seeds). Kill any min-delta near zero.

8. BASELINE + PREV CHAMP vs V14 (baseline edge must improve) AND vs the previous
                         champion (must win the crown back).

9. PROMOTE + REBUILD     Champion file with version/docstring patch; rebuild submission.py;
                         smoke + H2H the actual bundle.

10. SHIP + DOCUMENT      Submit to Kaggle; write results/<NEXT_STEP>.md following this
                         template; commit everything with the evidence files.
```

**Rules that made the difference (do not skip):**
- Deterministic engine → never explain away a delta; every game is evidence.
- Never fire a lever the tape already handles (dead-inventory filter) — avoid self-sabotage.
- Window beats trigger: the same lever failed at 570–704 and won at 705–716. Timing is the fix.
- Verify the *shipped artifact* (submission.py), not just the champion file.
