## half 2 - does wide-cliff weighting pay, at matched volume?

money ~ 1 + share(CARROT,TOMATO,MELON,STRAWBERRY) + log(tile-days) + n_animals
n=170 seat-episodes, R-squared=0.351  (STRAWBERRY included; WHEAT is the absorbed category)

**This is associational, not causal**: teams differ in opponent composition, so a positive
carrot coefficient can mark a rich team rather than a prize. Half 1 (ICC) is immune to that
confound and is the part that answers the question asked.
