## Market model (calibrated on replays + engine source, 1.32.6)
- price = base ± amp*shape(|inv-I0|), I0=10000, amp = target*base/shape(T).
- WOOL: base 200, below=+8.58*ln(1+x) (cap ~265), above=200-0.058x^2 → **floor at x=+59 above I0**.
- MILK: base 160, above linear 2.098/unit → floor at +76. STRAW floor at +62. MELON floor +158, FERT +500. WHEAT: log above (never floors).
- **WOOL realized px is yarn-store-gated** (108 live games):
  yarn=0: px 21.9 (n=38) | 1: 106.0 (n=42) | 2: 204.9 (n=21) | 3: 230 | 4-6: 243-254.
- Shop drain: every 4 turns/shop instance (YARN=2 wool/tick=12/day). Town center 1x all products every 24 turns. 8 shop instances drawn with replacement (1.32.6), unlock every 3 days.
- Animal economics: fed+cared animal yields 1+bonus per production event (bonus accrues +1/day fed&cared) → sheep ≈33 wool/season (first_yield_day 6, interval 3), cow ≈36 milk (day 8, interval 2). Both use PASTURE, both need 1 WHEAT/day FEED.
- WOOL px ~250 → sheep net ≈ +7k/season; MILK glutted (~$5-40 late) → cow ≈ 0-300. **Cow-heavy tape is obsolete when yarn>=1.**
- 1.32.6 additions: shed ops before LOCKED guard; BUY_PRODUCT/BUY_SEED blocked when shed>=100 (shedCapacity).
