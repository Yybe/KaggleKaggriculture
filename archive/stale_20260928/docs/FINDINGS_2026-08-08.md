# Findings — 2026-08-08 session (V23 vs Top-1 reference; endgame-transplant hypothesis)

## Question

Can V23 be improved by copying the #1-ranked agent's ("R1", Replays folder
`Wufang Hong`) late-game behavior? User-supplied prior: "R1 does 30-50 more
farmer actions than us." Goal of this session: verify that claim on hard
replay evidence, extract the divergence, and gate-test the transplant locally.

## Dataset & tooling

- 22 replay JSONs inspected → 16 with valid filled action streams → **32 parsed
  seats** (`analysis/parsed/*.pkl`).
  - `Wufang Hong` (R1): 15 games (30 seats), all `townCenterSellInterval=12`
    (LEGACY regime).
  - `rank 2-3` (Seb, www.sleepyai): 12 games (24 seats), LEGACY.
  - `my losses` (Chirantan Bhardwaj == our V23 submission): 5 games (10 seats),
    4 LEGACY + 1 REBALANCE (`interval=24`).
- Analysis pipeline: `analysis/parse_replay.py` → `metrics.py` → `build_table.py`
  → `classify.py` (per-seat profile) → `probe_identity.py` (match vs our
  `main.py` script family).
- Artifacts written: `analysis/table_classified.csv`, `analysis/identity_summary.txt`,
  `analysis/endgame_patches.pkl`, `analysis/gate_results.txt`.

## Result 1 — "R1 does 30-50 more farmer actions" is FALSIFIED

Identity probe (`_LEGACY_ACTIONS` vs each seat's farmer action stream):

- R1 matches our script family at **0.911**; it is the *same* deterministic
  pre-computed route family as V23, not a hand-driven ("active") agent.
- Per-seat farmer non-PASS actions: exact-script family **mean 542-544**;
  R1 mean **547** (fam-script mean 544). **Not 30-50 more — about 6 more,
  concentrated in the deterministic endgame.**
- Active (non-family) seats average 660 farmer non-pass actions but have
  LOWER mean final money (120,404) than the family seats (120,889) —
  action-count is not the differentiator.

## Result 2 — endgame divergence extracted and exactly reproduced

- Reference-family late-game differs from V23's in the last **64 steps**
  (R1 family, segmented day-20→) and **66 steps** (Mohammad/Burhan/Leon
  family, day-17→).
- Divergence within each Reference OUR seat is **perfectly deterministic**:
  the same deviation is bit-identical across all games of that agent. No
  opponent-conditioning observed in the endgame segment (only earlier in
  the day-0..d16 mid-game).
- Patches packaged: `analysis/endgame_patches.pkl` + three candidate wrappers
  (`candidate_A.py`, `candidate_AF.py`, `candidate_BF.py`):

## Conclusion

Keep V23 **unchanged**. All three empirically-derived transplant variants
lost in gates; the recorded losses vs the reference family are within
mirror noise; the farmer-action count claim is falsified at the replay
level. The reference's lead is not a learnable endgame route — it is a
**seat-level ±mirror coin-flip** once both sides converge on the same
scripted family.

## 2026-08-08 after-burn: Yybe contender tested locally (official engine 1.32.5)

New public contender: `github.com/Yybe/KaggleKaggriculture` (git HEAD
commit 014139d "V26: v23 + idle-turn fill + late-game high-value SELL
top-up"). Cloned to `contenders/YybeKaggriculture/`; entry points
`submission.py` (V26, HEAD) and `main.py` (V22, older).

**Contender provenance verified:** `submission.py` EMBEDS a full copy of
our V23 agent (`_V23_EMBEDDED`, inlined module) and adds two rules:
1. `_fill` — idle-turn fill (scheduled-synced, no-op in mirror);
2. `_topup` — from step >= 456 (final 10 days) AND only for high-value
   goods (STRAWBERRY 120 / MILK 160 / WOOL 200), bump each v23 SELL
   order by ≤ +2 shed units when shed > planned.
Local identity check: embedded v23 == our `main.py` **bit-identical
scores on seeds 4242/777 vs pass** (190,257 / 190,820 both sides).

**Gate (100 games, official 1.32.5, frozen decision seeds, 8 workers):**

| Matchup | result | mean Δ (cand−def) | 95% bootstrap CI | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V26 (`submission.py`) vs V23 (`main.py`) | 100/100 cand wins | **+618.6** | [+571.2, +664.5] | **PROMOTE** |
| V22 (`main.py` of repo) vs V23 | 49/50 def wins | −5,412.7 | [−5,985, −4,784] | REJECT |

**First PROMOTE of the session!** V26 beats V23 in 100/100 mirrors with a
narrow, statistically solid margin (+618, CI entirely > 0). The margin
comes exactly from the `_topup` rule: selling +1..+2 extra units of WOOL/
MILK/STRAWBERRY in the final 10 days. Note the mirror deltas and margins
are misleading (V26 inherits 100% of V23), so the H2H delta is not
"V23 beats V26" — the CI is entirely above zero.

**Caveat:** the stable PROMOTE is real but small (+0.17% of mean bank).
The effect is presumably in the shared-price shaping: V26 dumps slightly
MORE of the high-value products late, and since the game is a two-player
zero-sum price squeeze, bumping its OWN sell quantity past the plan lets
it out-play V23's fixed sell plan. Next question: is +618 stable across
more seeds (100 already tight) and does it transfer to the REBALANCE
regime (interval 24)? That gate is cheap and should be run before
adopting anything like this.
