## 3. Seat structure — the 64 games are really 32 measurements

`probeall` re-ran all 32 seeds and printed each seat's raw reward
([`results/t3_holdout.log`](results/t3_holdout.log), `PER-SEAT PROBE (32 seeds x 2 seats = 64 games …)`):

- **30 / 32 seeds produce an identical f90−V11 delta in both seats** — swapping player index does not
  change either agent's final coins, so the seat-symmetry break that AGENTS.md Rule 2 asks for never
  happens for 94 % of this bank. Effective independent sample = **32**, not 64; every "64-game" bank in
  the f90 evidence chain is half that.
- Only 2 seeds are genuinely seat-asymmetric: `40004000` (cand@P0 −3,672 / cand@P1 +3,925 → the +126.5
  mean is a 7,597-coin cancellation, i.e. a P0/P1 positional effect, not the graft) and `40028000`
  (−91 / +53). Both "wins" are therefore not attributable to the mechanism.
- The −1,296 outlier at `40029000` is seat-reproducible (f90 74,675 vs V11 75,971 in both seats) — a real
  world where the preemption loses 1.7 % of score, not a crash artifact.

## 5. Reading and consequence

f90's edge over V11 is **seed-bank luck, not a mechanism-level improvement**:

1. Sign test on the discriminating bank is a literal coin flip (15/15, p = 1.00). Pooling all four banks
   (62 decisive seeds) still fails significance at p = 0.213, and every larger bank moves the record
   further toward parity — the classic small-bank overfit signature.
2. The mean is positive only because of magnitude asymmetry (10 seeds ≥ +90 vs 1 seed ≤ −90). That
   asymmetry survives as a *hypothesis* worth keeping the graft for, but its own CI includes 0
   ([−36.8, +199.6]), and removing the single −1,296 world post hoc (mean → +132.3) is not admissible
   promotion evidence.
3. The stated refutation condition fires: a heavy negative tail (d ≤ −500) exists and is seat-reproducible.
4. Seat diagnostics remove the one seed whose win looked large (`40004000`) as a positional artifact.

**Action:** do not promote — `agents/pub_v57_apex_v11_dump.py` remains champion and `submission.py`
stays as-is. Do not gate further sell-timing/market overlays against this chassis on banks ≤ 16 seeds:
a 32-game bank cannot resolve an effect of this size (sd 346.5 → resolving ±88 at 80 % power needs
~120 independent seeds, and per Rule 3 seat duplication halves every bank's game count).
